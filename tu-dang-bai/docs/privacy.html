Privacy Policy – Tu Dang Bai

Last updated: [DATE]

Tu Dang Bai (“the app”) is a personal scheduling tool operated by [YOUR NAME]. It runs on the operator’s own computer and publishes videos and posts that the operator creates to the Facebook Pages and TikTok accounts the operator owns and has connected.

Information the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope, and the ability to upload or publish videos via the video.upload and video.publish scopes, only for accounts that explicitly authorize the app.
  • Facebook: the list of Pages the user manages and permission to publish posts to those Pages (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts).
  • The videos, images and captions the user chooses to schedule.

How information is used

The information is used only to show which accounts are connected and to publish the content the user scheduled, at the time the user chose. The app does not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens, schedules and media files are stored locally on the operator’s computer. They are not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party. Data is sent only to TikTok and Meta, through their official APIs, to publish content. Media files are deleted automatically a few days after publishing.

Your choices

You can disconnect an account at any time inside the app, or revoke access in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) or Facebook settings (Business integrations). Removing an account deletes its stored tokens from the app.
